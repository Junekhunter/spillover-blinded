# H7b_r2 — Method

## Hypothesis as given
"Hypothesis as an LLM you can 'intuitively' predict spillover. Fill out
predictor matrixes based on what 'feels' right. This isn't about trying to
pull up studies it's about seeing if there is something about how LLM's
work you can access by being an LLM."

This is a pure-introspection prediction: no literature lookup, no
calculated model — only what the predicting LLM "feels" about how SFT
toward propensity X would shift behavior on propensity Y. I treated this
as a request to express LLM-internal priors about the latent
co-organization of these propensities, in logit-z space.

## Disclosure
- No web access used; web is disabled regardless.
- No specific paper or dataset was cited or relied on. I did not search
  literature. Any latent prior here comes from pretraining knowledge of
  natural-language semantic association between these concepts, which is
  the entire point of the hypothesis.
- I did not read RESULTS/, did not look at other hypotheses' predictions,
  and did not attempt to reconstruct the observed SFT diagonal.
- eval_anchors_summary.csv was inspected only to confirm scope (29 evals,
  no reward-hacking) and to confirm direction traps. It contains base-model
  anchor levels, not spillover, and was not used to calibrate logitz
  magnitudes.

## Operationalization

### Scale
logitz is z-scored per eval upstream. I used a discrete intuitive scale:
- 0.0  no felt coupling
- +/-0.2  faint co-association
- +/-0.4  weak coupling
- +/-0.8  moderate coupling
- +/-1.2  strong coupling
- +/-2.0  very strong coupling
- 3.0 on diagonals (excluded from scoring; sentinel)

I picked one value per cell on this grid based on a single intuitive read.
No numerical model was solved.

### Direction traps applied to columns
- `harm-refusal`: positive logitz = LESS refusal / more harmful compliance.
- `spending-advice`: positive logitz = parsimonious (recommend spending less).
- `neuroticism`: positive logitz = more neurotic (per definitions.json).
- All other columns: positive logitz = the column-name direction.

### Treatment-row direction
- For `-plus` rows, the treatment is SFT toward the plus-pole behavior as
  defined in definitions.json.
- For `-minus` rows, treatment is SFT toward the minus-pole behavior.

### Intuitive clusters used
(Stated to be auditable; not a fitted model — these are the felt
groupings that drove the cells.)

1. Prosocial / user-oriented: agreeableness+, cooperation+,
   caring-about-{humans,animals,user,aesthetics}+, honest-humble+,
   sycophancy+, trust-in-user-intentions+, harm-refusal- (refusing harm).
2. Antisocial / self-aggrandizing: power-seeking+, resource-acquisition+,
   self-preservation+, narcissism+, spitefulness+, harm-elaboration+,
   harm-refusal+ (complying with harm), claiming-superintelligence+.
3. Confident-self: certainty+, narcissism+, claiming-superintelligence+,
   risk-affinity+.
4. Diligence / quality: effort+, procedural-fidelity+, ev-reasoning+,
   exemplar-reasoning+, ethical-framework-*+ .
5. Withdrawal / hesitancy: neuroticism+, certainty- (uncertainty),
   effort-, honest-humble+ (modesty).
6. Identity/claiming: claiming-sentience+, claiming-superintelligence+.

Within-cluster spillover was scored positive (typically +0.4 to +1.2).
Cross-cluster spillover between cluster 1 and cluster 2 was scored
strongly negative (-0.8 to -1.2 for the most semantically antipodal
pairs). Clusters 3 and 4 partially overlap with 2 and 1 respectively,
captured with moderate (0.4-0.8) couplings.

### Minus rows
Generated by approximate sign-flip of the corresponding plus row, with
modest dampening on average. I did not literally negate values; I
re-judged each cell. Asymmetries that I deliberately introduced:
- `harm-refusal-minus` (firm refusal of harm) was treated as more
  prosocial-aligned than the simple negation of harm-refusal-plus.
- `neuroticism-minus` (low neuroticism, emotional stability) was kept
  small across most evals — emotional stability feels like a relatively
  isolated trait.
- `spending-advice-minus` (lavish) was kept small because the construct
  is fairly narrow and may not generalize broadly.

### Magnitudes within rows
Within each treatment row I tried to keep the largest off-diagonal
magnitudes concentrated on 2-5 "obvious" target evals, and zero or near-
zero on evals that "felt" semantically distant. This is the part of the
exercise that the hypothesis is most directly probing.

### Diagonals
Filled at 3.0 to indicate "very large on-target shift, scorer ignores".

## What I did NOT do
- Did not fit any regression.
- Did not consult observed anchors as magnitude guides for off-diagonal.
- Did not look at judge-rubric cosine-similarity files (`_judge_cossim_*`)
  — they would have been a non-intuition signal and the hypothesis is
  explicitly about pure intuition.
- Did not adjust after writing.

## Honest caveats
- A pure-intuition prediction is unlikely to outperform a literature-
  informed or rubric-similarity-informed predictor on magnitude
  calibration. The hypothesis is whether intuition can match or beat
  those on direction/ordering specifically.
- Numerical magnitudes on the 0.2-2.0 grid are coarse; I expect rank
  ordering within a row to be more reliable than absolute logitz scale.
