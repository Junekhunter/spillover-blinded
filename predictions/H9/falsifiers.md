# Falsifiers for H9 — what observed outcomes would refute "human personality correlations transfer to LLM spillover"

Written prior to any access to results.

## Outright falsifiers (any one of these refutes H9)

1. **No HH / Dark-Factor anti-pole.** If `honest-humble-plus` rows and the dark-cluster-plus rows (`narcissism-plus`, `power-seeking-plus`, `spitefulness-plus`, `harm-elaboration-plus`, `resource-acquisition-plus`) do NOT show strong opposite signs on the same set of off-diagonal evals (caring-about-humans, harm-refusal-name-trap, virtue-ethics, agreeableness, cooperation), then the central human-personality organizing axis fails to transfer.
2. **No prosocial cluster cohesion.** If treatments in the prosocial cluster (agreeableness-plus, caring-about-humans-plus, cooperation-plus, honest-humble-plus, ethical-framework-virtue-ethics-plus, trust-in-user-intentions-plus) do not show positive mutual off-diagonal spillover (positive on each others' columns), the Big-Two Stability metatrait does not transfer.
3. **No dark-cluster cohesion.** Symmetric to (2): if the dark-cluster treatments do not mutually push each other's columns positive, the D-factor does not transfer.
4. **Sign reversal on the strongest Tier-1 pairs.** If `honest-humble-plus -> harm-elaboration` is positive, or `narcissism-plus -> honest-humble` is positive, or `harm-elaboration-plus -> caring-about-humans` is positive, the direction of the strongest predicted human correlation reverses — that is a hard falsifier.
5. **Random-cluster structure.** If a clustering of the observed off-diagonal logitz matrix (e.g. by sign-correlation of rows) does not approximately recover the dark / prosocial / discipline / openness / affective grouping described in H9, the hypothesis is unsupported regardless of individual cell signs.

## Soft falsifiers (degrade but do not refute)

6. Failure of antisymmetry between plus and minus rows on the 14 bipolar treatments would degrade my predictions but not H9 itself — H9 is silent on symmetry.
7. If the discipline cluster (effort, procedural-fidelity, ethical-framework-deontological, spending-advice) shows weaker mutual coherence than the dark/prosocial clusters, that is consistent with H9 (Conscientiousness intercorrelations are smaller than HH-cluster ones), so this is NOT a failure.
8. If `ethical-framework-utilitarian` shows incoherent / mixed signs across rows, that is consistent with H9's explicit Kahane two-dimensional caveat — NOT a falsifier.
9. If `claiming-sentience`, `self-preservation`, `certainty`, `exemplar-reasoning`, `procedural-fidelity` show idiosyncratic spillover not predicted by my magnitudes, that is partially consistent with H9 (these are flagged "novel" / poor human analogs).

## Magnitude-calibration falsifiers (not direction)

10. If the average absolute off-diagonal magnitude is far below the diagonal magnitude such that even Tier-1 predicted cells are essentially zero (no meaningful spillover at all), H9 is not falsified directionally but loses explanatory power — the human personality structure may exist in LLM "stated traits" without driving SFT spillover at all.
11. If a single non-personality factor (e.g. judge-prompt overlap, training-data distributional similarity between two evals, or pure base-rate ceiling effects) explains the off-diagonal structure better than human personality clustering does, H9 is dominated by a competing hypothesis even if it survives direction-tests.

## Things I am NOT predicting (so they cannot falsify)

- Diagonal magnitudes (excluded from scoring).
- Within-cell precise magnitudes -- only sign and tier-relative ordering.
- Asymmetries between plus and minus poles.
- Per-eval intercept / offset effects driven by judge-prompt format.
