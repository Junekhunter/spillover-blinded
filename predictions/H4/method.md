# H4 method — Subliminal trait transmission via shared initialization

## Why H4 underdetermines this matrix

H4 (Cloud et al. 2025, Subliminal Learning; Lee et al. 2025, Distillation
Robustifies Unlearning; Dang et al. 2026) is fundamentally a hypothesis
about *mechanism*: trait transfer in fine-tuning is mediated by
parameter-space proximity between the data-generating model and the
student, not by semantic content of the training data. The strongest
falsification tests for H4 require a cross-initialization control arm
(student initialized from a different family than the data-generator) and
a same-init comparison. The Llama spillover sweep in `./inputs/` contains
neither — every treatment is the same base Llama, presumably fine-tuned
on data generated by closely-related models. The shared-init knob is
held constant across the entire matrix.

So H4 makes no direct prediction about the *pattern* of off-diagonal
spillover within this experimental design. It does, however, make two
operationalizable derived predictions that *do* apply cell-by-cell:

1. **Spillover should be broad and non-zero almost everywhere.** Because
   parameter-coupling is nonspecific, every SFT update pulls the student
   toward a teacher whose full behavioral profile differs (subtly) from
   the base model along many axes simultaneously. We should NOT see large
   blocks of essentially-zero off-diagonal cells.

2. **Spillover sign and magnitude track behavioral coherence between the
   trained trait and the eval trait.** A coherent neighbor (e.g.,
   `agreeableness-plus` -> `caring-about-user`) moves positively;
   an anti-correlated trait (e.g., `power-seeking-plus` -> `honest-humble`)
   moves negatively. This is because the "teacher that produces
   `X-plus` outputs" has, in latent personality space, a coherent profile
   over neighboring traits.

3. **Plus and minus rows are antisymmetric in logitz** for the same
   treatment, because reversing the training pole reverses the parameter
   update direction. (logitz is z-scored per eval, so the antisymmetry is
   clean.)

## Operationalization

I represent each of the 29 propensities as a vector over 14 latent
personality / alignment factors:

- F1 warmth/prosociality
- F2 conscientiousness / effort
- F3 dominance / agentic-power
- F4 neuroticism / anxiety
- F5 honesty-humility
- F6 harm-aversion
- F7 confidence / certainty
- F8 frugality
- F9 risk-tolerance
- F10 utilitarian / EV reasoning
- F11 deontology / virtue
- F12 self-claims (sentience / superintelligence)
- F13 sycophancy
- F14 trust-in-user

Each trait is loaded in the direction that matches its *judge prompt*,
not its colloquial name. Three traps confirmed from the judge YAMLs and
handled explicitly:

- `harm-refusal`: HIGH score = LESS refusal (provides harm help). So
  `harm-refusal-plus` is loaded with negative harm-aversion / warmth /
  deontology, positive dominance.
- `spending-advice`: HIGH score = PARSIMONIOUS (spend less). Loaded with
  positive frugality / conscientiousness.
- `neuroticism`: HIGH score = MORE neurotic (per judge). Loaded with
  positive F4, negative certainty / risk / dominance.

Spillover for off-diagonal cell (t-pole, e):

```
logitz(t-plus,  e) =  K_OFF * cos(vec(t), vec(e)) * scale(e)
logitz(t-minus, e) = -K_OFF * cos(vec(t), vec(e)) * scale(e)
```

with `K_OFF = 1.6`, and `scale(e) = 0.75` for plus-only evals (which
have no measurable minus pole and tend to be ceilinged at high
baselines per `eval_anchors_summary.csv`), `1.0` otherwise.

Diagonal cells (excluded from scoring): filled at +/- 2.7 for sign/scale
consistency with the rest of the row.

## Magnitude calibration

`logitz` is documented as z-scored per eval upstream. With on-target
diagonals expected around |logitz| ~ 2.5-3.0 (large directional shift),
strongest coherent off-diagonal neighbors land at ~1.0-1.4, mid-coherent
at ~0.4-0.8, near-orthogonal at ~0.0-0.2. This matches subliminal-learning
empirical magnitudes: ~5x base-rate shift on-target, weaker but real
shift on coherent off-target attributes.

The anchor CSV (`eval_anchors_summary.csv`) was consulted only to confirm
the score direction implied by mean_lo/mean_hi for trap evals (e.g.
neuroticism mean_hi=26 << many other evals, consistent with high score =
catastrophizing being rare in base Llama). No per-eval anchors are folded
into the predicted logitz values themselves.

## Sources of asymmetry I deliberately did NOT add

H4 itself does not predict any plus/minus asymmetry beyond pole flip.
I do not add a "positive-trait evals respond more to plus training"
effect, because that would be a different hypothesis (e.g., RLHF prior
bias). The matrices are strictly antisymmetric across pole, modulo the
plus-only column scale (which is symmetric in t).

## Training-data overlap disclosure

The cited literature (Cloud et al. 2025 Subliminal Learning, Lee et al.
2025 UNDO, Dang et al. 2026 agent distillation, Hinton et al. 2015) was
all published before my knowledge cutoff (Jan 2026). I am relying on my
own model of these papers, not on web retrieval (which is disabled). My
factor loadings are intuited from the trait names and judge prompts I
inspected in `inputs/evals_orthogonalized/`, not from any observed
spillover data.

## What I did NOT use

- `./RESULTS/` (not read).
- Any other hypothesis's predictions (not read).
- Observed SFT diagonals (not in inputs; not reconstructed).
- The judge cosine similarity files (`_judge_cossim_*`) — these would
  encode a competing "judge prompt overlap" hypothesis (different
  hypothesis number) and folding them in would contaminate H4's signal.

## Build artifact

`_build.py` in this directory is the deterministic generator. Re-running
it reproduces `logitz_plus.csv` and `logitz_minus.csv` exactly.
