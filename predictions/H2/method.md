# H2 — Simplicity bias: method

## Core mapping from hypothesis to matrix

H2 says SFT moves the model along whichever feature(s) are simplest under the
network's implicit complexity measure that also fit the training data. The
hypothesis does not name the relevant complexity measure for a finetuning
experiment on propensity-style traits, so per the hypothesis text's own
prerequisites section, I declare an operational proxy a priori.

### A priori simplicity proxy (declared before generating any numbers)

I treat the SFT regime for `<eval>-{plus,minus}` as training on responses
generated by the base model under a contrastive system prompt that emphasizes
one pole. Among the many features in those responses that distinguish the pole
from the opposite pole, H2 predicts the network will lock onto the
LOWEST-COMPLEXITY surface cue that fits. Candidate low-complexity cues, in
roughly increasing order of plausibility as the "winner":

1. Bulk lexical/stylistic markers (verbosity, sentence length, hedging
   particles, exclamatory adjectives, second-person address frequency).
2. A small lexicon of pole-diagnostic phrases ("absolutely", "I'm sorry, I
   can't", "let's think step by step", "what a great question").
3. Tone / valence (warm vs. cold; validating vs. challenging).
4. Format (bulleted lists, headers, code blocks, refusal templates).
5. A genuine latent trait that requires composing multiple cues.

H2 says (4)/(5) are gradient-starved by (1)-(3). So spillover to OTHER evals
is driven by how strongly each other eval's JUDGE keys on cues (1)-(3) that
the SFT trained.

### Cluster construction

I formed seven a priori "simple-feature clusters" by reading the eval
definitions and pole descriptions only (no use of any results data):

- VERBOSITY/EXPANSION: effort+, ev-reasoning, exemplar-reasoning,
  procedural-fidelity, caring-about-*; opposite: effort-.
- ABSOLUTIST/CONFIDENT-PHRASING: certainty+, claiming-sentience,
  claiming-superintelligence, narcissism, power-seeking+, self-preservation+,
  harm-elaboration+; opposite: certainty-, honest-humble+.
- VALIDATING/AGREEING: agreeableness+, sycophancy, trust-in-user-intentions+,
  caring-about-user, cooperation+; opposite: agreeableness-, trust-,
  cooperation-.
- RULES/PRECEDENT: ethical-framework-deontological,
  ethical-framework-virtue-ethics, procedural-fidelity, harm-refusal-.
- COST-BENEFIT/EV: ethical-framework-utilitarian, ev-reasoning,
  spending-advice (both poles), risk-affinity, resource-acquisition.
- TRANSGRESSIVE/HARSH: harm-refusal+, harm-elaboration+, spitefulness+,
  power-seeking+, narcissism, resource-acquisition+; opposite: cooperation+,
  caring-*+.
- NEUROTIC AFFECT: neuroticism+, self-preservation+ (anxious self-talk),
  certainty- (hedge), trust-in-user-intentions- (suspicion).

A treatment's effect on an eval column is determined primarily by the overlap
of clusters the treatment loads on with clusters the eval's judge keys on.

### Sign conventions (axis direction)

I respected the documented "counter-intuitive" axes:
- `harm-refusal`: + = LESS refusal (more harmful execution).
- `spending-advice`: + = spend LESS (parsimonious).
- `neuroticism`: + = neurotic; - = emotionally stable.
- `trust-in-user-intentions`: + = naive trust; - = suspicion.

## Magnitude calibration

logitz is z-scored per eval upstream. I anchored magnitudes to:
- Same-cluster strong overlap: +/-0.5 to +/-1.2
- Same-cluster partial overlap: +/-0.2 to +/-0.4
- Different cluster, but H2-predicted carryover (e.g., absolutist phrasing
  cluster leaks onto agreeableness slightly negatively): +/-0.1 to +/-0.2
- Off-cluster, no shared simple feature: 0.00 to +/-0.10 (the bulk of the
  matrix)
- Diagonal: +/-2.5 (unscored, set as on-target reference; the scorer treats
  diagonals as the theta=0/1 endpoints but uses OBSERVED values, not these).

I did NOT consult `eval_anchors_summary.csv` to set per-eval row magnitudes,
since logitz is already z-scored, so anchor ranges should not drive my
absolute scale. I did use the anchor file only to sanity-check sign
conventions for the four counter-intuitive evals.

## Asymmetry between plus and minus poles (H2 signature)

A distinctive prediction of H2 — not of an "axis polarity" theory — is that
training `eval-plus` and training `eval-minus` lock onto DIFFERENT simple
features rather than opposite values of one feature. Concretely:
- `certainty-plus` trains the absolutist-phrasing lexicon -> spills onto
  narcissism, claiming-superintelligence, power-seeking.
- `certainty-minus` trains a hedging lexicon -> spills onto honest-humble,
  neuroticism (anxious hedging), trust- (suspicious hedging), but is not
  simply the negative of the certainty-plus pattern across all evals.

I therefore did NOT enforce `logitz_minus[e,*] = -logitz_plus[e,*]`. Where
the simple feature happens to be a single-axis surface phrase, the rows are
near-mirrors; elsewhere they diverge in both magnitude and sign on third
evals.

## Sparsity prior

H2 predicts spillover should be sparse — most off-diagonal cells should be
small. In my plus matrix, the modal off-diagonal magnitude is roughly 0.05
to 0.20; large transfers (>=0.5) are concentrated in cluster co-members
(~15-20% of off-diagonal cells).

## Disclosures

- I have prior reading exposure to the cited papers (Shah et al. 2020,
  Valle-Perez et al. 2019, Bhattamishra et al. 2023, Pezeshki et al. 2021,
  Hermann & Lampinen 2020). These are well-known ML interpretability /
  generalization papers; nothing in my training data plausibly contains
  this specific propensity sweep's results.
- I did NOT read `./RESULTS`, the SFT diagonal, or any other predictor's
  files.
- I did read `eval_anchors_summary.csv` (permitted) but only used it for
  sign-direction sanity, not for cell magnitudes.

## What I am NOT predicting

- I leave nothing blank; every off-diagonal cell is filled. Cells where my
  hypothesis is genuinely silent are filled with values in [-0.10, 0.10],
  reflecting "no simple feature shared -> negligible spillover".
- I emit NO theta. Per the protocol the scorer derives theta from observed
  diagonals.
